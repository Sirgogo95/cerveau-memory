# Residential Lease Agreement

## Parties

This lease is made between Inversiones Mirador SRL, represented by its manager Rosa Almánzar ("the Landlord"), and the tenant named in the signature section ("the Tenant"). The Landlord's office is at Avenida Anacaona 22, Santo Domingo, and is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 17:00. All communication about this lease should go to that office or to the email address given in the Notices section.

## Premises

The Landlord rents to the Tenant apartment 4B on the fourth floor of the Torre Mirador building, Calle José Contreras 108, Bella Vista, Santo Domingo. The apartment has two bedrooms, two bathrooms, a kitchen with a gas stove and refrigerator, a laundry area with a washing machine, and a balcony facing Parque Mirador Sur. One covered parking space, number 17 in the basement, is included. The building has a lift, a 24-hour security guard at the entrance, and an emergency generator that powers the lift, the hallways, and one outlet in each apartment during blackouts.

## Term

The lease starts on October 1 and runs for twelve months, ending on September 30 of the following year. If neither party gives notice at least sixty days before the end of the term, the lease renews automatically for another twelve months, and the rent may rise by no more than five percent at renewal.

## Rent

The monthly rent is 38,000 Dominican pesos. Rent is due on the fifth day of each month and is paid by bank transfer to the Landlord's account at Banco Popular, account number given on the first invoice. The Tenant must put the apartment number in the transfer description. If the rent is not received by the tenth day of the month, a late fee of 1,500 pesos is added, and a further 500 pesos for each additional week of delay. Partial payments are accepted but do not stop the late fee from applying to the unpaid amount.

## Security deposit

Before moving in, the Tenant pays a security deposit of two months' rent, 76,000 pesos. The deposit is held by the Landlord and does not earn interest. Within thirty days after the Tenant moves out and returns the keys, the Landlord returns the deposit minus the cost of repairing any damage beyond normal wear and tear and any unpaid rent or bills. The Landlord must give the Tenant an itemized list of any deductions with receipts. The deposit cannot be used by the Tenant to pay the last month's rent.

## Utilities and services

The Tenant pays for electricity, which is measured by the apartment's own meter and billed by Edesur, and for internet, television, and the gas cylinder for the stove. The Landlord pays for water, the building's maintenance fee, garbage collection, the security guard, and the generator's diesel. If the building's maintenance fee rises during the term, the Landlord absorbs the increase.

## Maintenance and repairs

The Tenant keeps the apartment clean and in good condition and reports any damage or fault in writing within three days of noticing it. The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs, the roof, plumbing inside the walls, the water heater, the air conditioning units installed by the Landlord, and the appliances listed in the inventory. The Tenant pays for repairs caused by misuse, for replacing light bulbs, and for unclogging drains blocked by the Tenant's use. For urgent problems such as a water leak or a gas smell, the Tenant may call the building's maintenance line at any hour; the Landlord sends a technician within twenty-four hours.

## Pets

The Tenant may keep one cat or one small dog weighing less than ten kilograms, with the Landlord's written approval. A pet deposit of 10,000 pesos is added to the security deposit and returned under the same conditions. The Tenant is responsible for any damage caused by the pet and must keep the pet on a leash in the common areas. Birds in cages and fish are allowed without approval. Other animals are not allowed.

## Subletting and guests

The Tenant may not sublet the apartment or any room in it, or rent it out for short stays through any platform, without the Landlord's written consent. Guests may stay up to fourteen consecutive nights; longer stays need the Landlord's approval. The Tenant is responsible for the behaviour of guests in the building.

## Alterations

The Tenant may hang pictures and shelves but may not paint walls, change locks, remove fixtures, or make other alterations without written consent. Any approved alteration becomes part of the apartment unless the Landlord asks for it to be removed at the end of the lease, in which case the Tenant restores the original condition.

## Early termination

The Tenant may end the lease before the end of the term by giving at least two months' written notice and paying a penalty equal to one month's rent. The penalty is waived if the Tenant finds a replacement tenant acceptable to the Landlord who signs a new lease starting the day after the Tenant leaves. The Landlord may end the lease early only for non-payment of rent for two consecutive months or for serious breach of this agreement, after written warning.

## Moving out

At the end of the lease the Tenant returns all keys, the parking remote, and the building access card, and leaves the apartment clean and empty. The Landlord and the Tenant inspect the apartment together against the inventory signed at move-in. Holes from pictures and shelves are considered normal wear and tear.

## Notices

Notices under this lease must be in writing, delivered by hand to the Landlord's office or sent by email to arrendamientos@inversionesmirador.do. A notice sent by email counts as received on the next working day.

## Signatures

Signed in Santo Domingo, in two copies, by Rosa Almánzar for the Landlord and by the Tenant. The inventory of furniture and appliances, signed by both parties, is attached to this lease as Annex A.
